\section{Conclusion}

We presented \textbf{StructFlow}, a methodology for compiling Probabilistic Graphical Models into task-specific Block-Triangular Flows: a \textit{Causal Alignment} model preserving the generative factorization for interventional and counterfactual queries, or an \textit{Inference Alignment} model capturing posterior correlations.

\cam{Our evidence comes from synthetic graphs and image benchmarks with checkable answers; real-world applicability remains to be demonstrated (Section~\ref{sec:future_work}).} Two conclusions follow. First, the compiler abstraction earns its keep: one specification compiles to answer interventional, counterfactual, and amortized posterior queries correctly \rev{(co-parent dependencies for colliders; tractable prefix queries)}. Second, \cam{dynamic conditioning's parallel sampling is not free: it is consistent only when noise and data are paired block by block, and even then its error on deep chains grows with depth in a way solver effort does not repair, while static conditioning stays accurate (Section~\ref{sec:chain-stiffness}).}
